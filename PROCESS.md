## Basic Process

1. Conceptualize your Idea
2. Create requirements
3. Revise the requirements from expertise
4. Convert the requirements into atomic tasks.
5. Set up guardrails.
6. Supervise.

## General Tips

1. Be clear.
2. Review output!!
3. Be as interactive as you'd like.
4. Don't outsource thinking if you don't have to.
